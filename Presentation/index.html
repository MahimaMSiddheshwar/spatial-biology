01 · Background & Question

Why where matters
as much as what

The central question: why do immune cells fail to penetrate ER+ breast cancer tumour nests — and can we spatially map the molecular barrier responsible? Bulk RNA-seq cannot answer this. Spatial transcriptomics can.

The Problem
Bulk RNA-seq Destroys Spatial Information
Traditional bulk RNA-seq averages gene expression across thousands of cells — completely destroying spatial context. A tumour is not uniform: the cancer cell nests, the fibrous stroma, the immune exclusion boundary, and the vascular regions all have completely different transcriptional programmes. Averaging them produces a signal that faithfully represents none of these compartments. The question of where becomes unanswerable.
The Solution
Spatial Transcriptomics Preserves Location
10x Genomics Visium captures genome-wide gene expression at every 55 µm spot while preserving exact XY coordinates on the tissue section. Each spot captures ~8 cells. This lets us ask: which genes are spatially concentrated? Which cell types co-localise? Where do immune checkpoints activate? Where does TGF-β barrier signalling occur? Questions that fundamentally require knowing where — not just what.
Research Objectives
O1 → Identify 11 transcriptionally distinct spatial TME domains using Leiden clustering
O2 → Detect 2,493 spatially variable genes via Moran's I spatial autocorrelation
O3 → Score 24 cell type signatures per spot using Wu et al. 2021 scRNA-seq atlas
O4 → Detect Tertiary Lymphoid Structures using 3 independent analytical methods
O5 → Spatially map the dual immunosuppressive barrier (ECM + checkpoint signaling)
O6 → Confirm ER+ Luminal A subtype via PAM50 subtyping and PROGENy pathway scoring

NB02 · Quality Control & Preprocessing

From Raw Counts to
Analysis-Ready Data

Six sequential preprocessing steps applied in the correct biological order — each with a specific statistical justification. Raw counts preserved in adata.layers['counts'] and adata.raw for all downstream scoring.

1
Quality Filtering
Four filters applied: minimum 200 genes/spot (removes empty or dying spots), minimum 500 UMI/spot (ensures reliable quantification), maximum 15% MT% (excludes lysed cells releasing mitochondrial RNA), and genes detected in at least 3 spots (removes ultra-rare artefacts).
WHY 15% MT% CUTOFF: MT% > 15% indicates a cell that has lost its cytoplasmic membrane and is releasing mitochondrial RNA — a sign of cell death, not biology. The 3-cell minimum for genes removes PCR amplification noise masquerading as real expression.
RESULT: 4,898 → 4,869 spots (99.4% retention) · 36,601 → 21,349 genes retained
2
Library Size Normalisation (10k CPM)
Each spot's counts normalised to a total of 10,000 — removing the effect of sequencing depth differences between spots. Without normalisation, a spot with 20,000 UMIs would appear to have double the expression of a spot with 10,000 UMIs, even if the underlying biology is identical.
WHY BEFORE LOG: The normalisation assumes a linear relationship between counts. Log-transforming first would violate this assumption.
sc.pp.normalize_total(adata, target_sum=1e4)
3
log1p Transformation
Natural ln(x+1) transformation applied. Compresses the dynamic range of highly expressed genes. Without this, PCA would be completely dominated by the highest-expressed genes, obscuring the biologically meaningful variation in moderately expressed genes.
X range after: min 0.000, max 7.975 — log-normal distribution confirmed
4
Highly Variable Gene Selection (3,000 HVGs)
Select 3,000 genes with highest biological variation relative to technical noise using the Seurat v3 method. This reduces the 21,349-gene matrix to 3,000 genes where the signal-to-noise ratio is highest. PCA is then computed only on these genes.
WHY 3,000: Fewer HVGs (e.g. 500) miss rare cell type markers. More (e.g. 5,000) include genes with mostly technical variation. 3,000 is empirically validated in the field for Visium data.
3,000 / 21,349 genes selected (14%) · Used for PCA + clustering only
5
Scaling to Unit Variance
Zero-mean, unit-variance scaling applied to HVGs only before PCA. Prevents high-expression genes from dominating principal components simply because of scale. Applied only for PCA — log-normalised values are kept in adata.raw for all downstream gene scoring.
Scaled range: min –10.0, max +10.0 (clipped at 10 to limit outlier influence)
6
PCA → kNN Graph → UMAP → Leiden
PCA computed (50 components). Top 30 PCs selected from elbow plot — explaining 16.9% of variance (normal for spatial transcriptomics). kNN graph built (k=15, 30 PCs). UMAP embedding for visualisation. Leiden clustering tested at resolutions 0.3, 0.5, 0.6, 1.0 — resolution 0.5 selected (11 clusters, validated by H&E overlay).
kNN graph: 28,578 non-zero connections across 4,869 spots · UMAP stored in adata.obsm['X_umap']

NB04 · Spatial Analysis

Moran's I, Neighbourhood Enrichment
& Spatially Variable Genes

For each of 3,000 HVGs: does high expression concentrate in specific tissue regions, or is it randomly scattered? Moran's I answers this — 2,493 genes are significantly non-random (FDR < 0.05).

What is Moran's I? A spatial autocorrelation statistic ranging from +1 (perfectly spatially clustered) to 0 (completely random) to −1 (spatially dispersed checkerboard pattern). For each gene, 100 permutations of spot labels test whether the observed clustering is greater than chance. Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction applied across 3,000 simultaneous tests — chosen over Bonferroni because Bonferroni is too conservative, inflating false negatives when testing thousands of genes.

Central Finding

🔒 Lock 1 — Physical Wall (ECM)
FN1 → ITGB1 / ITGA5
Fibronectin (FN1) binds to integrin receptors (ITGB1/ITGA5) to form a dense extracellular matrix mesh. This physically anchors cells and creates a stromal barrier that prevents immune cell migration toward the tumour core. POSTN and COL1A1 reinforce this structural wall.
Top ECM score: FN1→ITGB1 = 0.98 (highest LR pair in the dataset)
🔒 Lock 2 — Chemical Shield (Checkpoint)
LAG3 → MHC-II + PD-L1 → PD-1
LAG3 binds to MHC-II (on Macrophages/APCs) to exhaust T-cells at the tumour boundary. PD-L1/PD-1 provides additional checkpoint inhibition. The immune system is recruited but chemically silenced at the same location — the Macrophage-rich boundary cluster.
Checkpoint score at boundary: LAG3→MHC-II = 0.50
Why both locks are simultaneously active: The pathway heatmap shows both ECM/Adhesion and Immune Checkpoint pathways scoring at maximum in the same boundary cluster. The Physical Wall prevents migration. The Chemical Shield prevents activation. This dual mechanism explains why monotherapy against either lock alone is insufficient.

08 · Key Findings

Three Biological Discoveries
From One Tissue Section

Each finding confirmed by multiple independent analytical methods — not a single pipeline artefact but convergent evidence from Moran's I, cell scoring, and ligand-receptor communication.

1
Tertiary Lymphoid Structure Detected — Three Independent Confirmations
A Tertiary Lymphoid Structure (TLS) — an organised lymph node equivalent forming inside the tumour — was detected by three completely independent methods, all pointing to the same upper-left perivascular tissue zone. TLS presence in breast cancer is associated with improved patient survival. This is not a subtle signal — immunoglobulins IGLC2, IGHG3, and IGKC are the 2nd, 4th, and 6th most spatially concentrated genes in the entire dataset.
Method 1: Moran's I — IGLC2 (I=0.815), IGHG3 (I=0.793), IGKC (I=0.773) — ranks 2, 4, 6 of 3,000 genes | all plasma cell immunoglobulins | FDR=0.0126 | 100 permutations
Method 2: Cell scoring — Plasma cell score peaks at 2.34 (highest immune signature) | B cells co-enriched same zone | TLS signature confirmed
Method 3: LR communication — CXCL13→CXCR5 confirmed (500 perms, p<0.001) | CXCL13 = the canonical TLS-building chemokine
2
Dual Immunosuppressive Barrier Identified — The Mechanism of Immune Exclusion
Two molecular locks simultaneously block immune cell entry into the tumour. Lock 1: ECM/Adhesion (FN1→ITGB1, score=0.98) creates a physical stromal wall. Lock 2: Checkpoint (LAG3→MHC-II, score=0.50) chemically exhausts T-cells. Both are maximally active simultaneously at the Macrophages/APC boundary. Every cytotoxic immune cell type scores zero in the ER+ Invasive Carcinoma cluster.
Physical Wall: FN1→ITGB1 (0.98), FN1→ITGA5 (0.32), POSTN→ITGAV (0.26) | ECM pathway score = 1.00
Chemical Shield: LAG3→MHC-II (0.50), PD-L1→PD-1 (0.01), TGFβ1→TGFBR2 (0.22) | Checkpoint pathway score = 1.00
Cross-validation: PROGENy TGF-β peaks in Macrophages/APC cluster (1.00) independently confirming Lock 1
Clinical: Explains poor PD-1 monotherapy response in ER+ breast cancer. Supports combination therapy.
3
ER+ Luminal A Subtype Confirmed Spatially — Cross-Validated by Two Independent Methods
The ER+ Luminal A molecular subtype was confirmed spatially using PAM50 gene signature scoring (Luminal A = 2,815 spots, 58%) and independently cross-validated by PROGENy pathway footprint analysis. HER2-enriched (421 spots) and Basal-like (493 spots) are present at lower levels. The GATA3 master transcription factor is the top marker for cluster C6.
PAM50: Luminal A dominant (2,815 spots, 58%) | HER2-enriched = 421 spots | Basal-like = 493 spots
Cluster markers: SLC39A6, TMEM150C, TNFSF10, BAMBI — all consistent with ER+ luminal programme
Clinical: Hormone therapy (tamoxifen/aromatase inhibitors) indicated. Explains poor immunotherapy response.

Complete Biological Story

Step 1
ER+ Tumour Forms Dense Nests
CCND1+, GATA3+, ESR1+ carcinoma cells growing in dense invasive nests. PAM50 Luminal A confirmed. High proliferation index.
Step 2
CAFs Build the Stromal Wall
MGP (#1 SVG), COL1A1, FN1 construct fibrous stroma around every nest. TGF-β secreted. Physical barrier assembled.
Step 3
TLS Forms at Periphery
Plasma cells + B cells organise near blood vessels. CXCL13→CXCR5 drives recruitment. Immune system builds a base camp.
Step 4

Honest acknowledgement of current limitations — and the six specific next steps that would strengthen and extend these findings.

Current Limitations
Single patient: Findings require multi-patient cohort validation before generalisation to the ER+ Luminal A population.

55 µm resolution: Each spot captures ~8 cells. Cell types inferred from signatures, not directly observed at single-cell level.

Signature scoring ≠ deconvolution: sc.tl.score_genes gives relative enrichment, not absolute cell counts.

Correlation ≠ causation: LR co-expression suggests signalling. Functional validation required.
